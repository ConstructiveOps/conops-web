## Who we are

This notice covers constructiveoperations.com and sundialcrm.com, operated by Constructive Operations LLC, PO Box 67015, Phoenix, AZ 85082.

## Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies and does not identify individual visitors.

We do not use advertising pixels or cross-site tracking.

## Contact form

When you send us a message, we receive your name, company, email, phone number, message, the page you were on, and whether you agreed to text messages. These go straight to our customer records system (Salesforce) so we can reply. Our website does not keep a copy.

## Chat assistant

Our chat assistant is powered by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Your messages are sent to Anthropic to generate replies, are not used to train their models, and are kept by Anthropic for a limited period under its commercial terms.

The conversation is kept only in your browser until you close the tab. If you ask us to follow up and give your name and contact details, we create a record in Salesforce with those details and a short summary of the conversation.

The assistant answers from the content of this website. It can make mistakes, so please confirm anything important with our team.

## Limits

To prevent abuse we count requests from each connection using a scrambled, anonymous code that changes every day. The counts are deleted automatically within 24 hours.

## Text messages

If you tick the text-message box, you agree to receive text messages from Constructive Operations about your request at the number you gave. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Agreeing is not a condition of any purchase. We do not sell or share your mobile number or your text-messaging consent with third parties for their marketing.
